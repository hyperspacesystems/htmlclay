Run local programs from your HTML files

Sandboxed by default. Explicit when a file asks for more.

Every .htmlclay page runs inside your regular browser's sandbox on 127.0.0.1. An unknown download opens read-only on its own isolated origin and cannot touch other files or run local programs behind your back.

  • Pinned program approvals: native dialogs show the exact script path, pin its binary identity before running, and have no default Return-key button.
  • Built-in data-loss guard: unexpected outside edits or large deletions keep a pinned backup in Kept Copies with one-click restore.
  • Nothing leaves your machine unasked: zero analytics, no CORS headers to outside sites, and never serves files outside your home folder.
